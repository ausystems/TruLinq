The anatomy of a stamp.

Four checks, in the order a reviewer runs them. All four pass, or the stamp is not issued.

01

Identity

A government ID is matched to the person behind the account.

02

Business

Registration numbers are checked against public business registries.

03

Presence

Website, domain and business contact details are confirmed as active.

04

Review

A human reviewer signs off before the stamp is issued.

a person, not a model

What the stamp means.

A Trulinq Verified stamp means we confirmed the person’s identity and the existence of their business at the time of review.

It means

  • We confirmed the person’s identity at the time of review
  • We confirmed the business exists and is linked to them
  • A human signed off
  • It can be revoked at any time

It does not mean

  • A guarantee of business performance
  • A guarantee of product quality
  • A guarantee of the outcome of any deal
  • Financial advice

Always use your own judgment and written contracts.
